# Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance across different outlet types, outlet sizes, locations, item categories, and fat-content segments.

The dashboard converts raw sales data into actionable business insights by tracking key performance indicators such as total sales, average sales, number of items, and average customer rating.

The analysis focuses on understanding sales drivers, outlet performance, product-category contribution, and geographic patterns to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales: $1.2M
- Average Sales: $141
- Number of Items: 8,523
- Average Rating: 3.9

## Key Business Insights
## Key Insights

### 1. Supermarket Type 1 is the dominant sales contributor

Supermarket Type 1 generated approximately $787.5K in sales, significantly higher than the other outlet types.

This indicates that Supermarket Type 1 outlets are the strongest contributor to overall sales performance.

### 2. Larger outlets generate higher sales

High-size outlets generated approximately $507.9K in sales, followed by medium outlets at approximately $444.8K and small outlets at approximately $249K.

This suggests a positive relationship between outlet size and sales generation.

### 3. Tier 3 locations generated the highest sales

Tier 3 locations contributed approximately $472.1K in sales, ahead of Tier 2 ($393.2K) and Tier 1 ($336.4K).

This indicates that lower-tier markets represent a significant opportunity for sales growth.

### 4. Regular-fat products contribute more sales

Regular-fat products generated approximately $776.3K in sales compared with approximately $425.4K from low-fat products.

Regular-fat products therefore account for the larger share of total sales in the analyzed dataset.

### 5. Fruits and snacks are among the strongest product categories

Fruits and snacks each contributed approximately $180K in sales, making them the leading item categories in the dashboard.

This highlights these categories as important contributors to overall product performance.

### 6. Outlet establishment peaked around 2018

The dashboard shows a significant increase in outlet sales around 2018, reaching approximately $205K, followed by a decline in subsequent years.

This suggests that outlet establishment and performance changed considerably over the period and may warrant further investigation.

### 7. Overall business performance

The dashboard reports total sales of approximately $1.2M across 8,523 items, with an average sales value of $141 and an average rating of 3.9.

The analysis highlights outlet type, outlet size, location tier, and product category as important dimensions for understanding sales performance.



## Business Takeaways

- Prioritize high-performing Supermarket Type 1 outlets while identifying factors driving their stronger performance.
- Evaluate expansion opportunities in Tier 3 markets given their higher sales contribution.
- Investigate the relationship between outlet size and sales to optimize store formats.
- Use product-category performance to improve inventory allocation and assortment planning.
- Monitor the changing contribution of low-fat and regular-fat products to identify evolving consumer preferences.
